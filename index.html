Privacy Policy for Shadowcraft 3D

Last updated: November 20, 2026

Welcome to Shadowcraft 3D ("we," "our," or "us"). We are committed to protecting your privacy and providing a safe, enjoyable mobile gaming experience. This Privacy Policy explains how information is collected, used, and disclosed when you play Shadowcraft 3D.

Summary: Shadowcraft 3D is an offline-first 3D puzzle game. We do not collect your name, email, phone number, location, or private contacts. All game progress and unlocked achievements are stored locally on your device. We use third-party services (Google AdMob and Google Play In-App Billing) to provide advertisements and digital purchases.

1. Information We Collect

We do not directly collect or store any personally identifiable information (PII). However, third-party SDKs integrated into the game may automatically collect certain device data:

  • Device Information: Device model, operating system version, screen resolution, and language settings.
  • Advertising & Analytics Identifiers: Google Advertising ID (GAID) used by Google AdMob to serve non-personalized or personalized rewarded advertisements.
  • Game Progress Data: Stored locally on your device (levels cleared, celestial shards balance, custom settings).

2. Third-Party Services & SDKs

Our app incorporates trusted third-party SDKs that operate under their own privacy policies:

  • Google AdMob (Mobile Ads SDK): We use Google AdMob to display rewarded video advertisements. AdMob may collect and process pseudonymous identifiers and device data in accordance with Google's policies.
    Google Privacy Policy | Google AdMob Policies
  • Google Play Billing (In-App Purchases): All purchases (VIP Masterpiece Pass, Celestial Shard packs) are securely processed directly by Google Play. We never receive or store your credit/debit card numbers or financial credentials.

3. How We Use Information

Any data processed by third-party services is used solely for:

  • Delivering rewarded video ads when requested by the player.
  • Preventing fraudulent ad impressions and ensuring network security.
  • Processing in-app purchases and restoring purchases.
  • Maintaining stability and performance of the game.

4. Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

Shadowcraft 3D does not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under the age of 13. If you believe that your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us immediately, and we will take appropriate measures to delete such data.

5. Data Retention & Security

All player progress, scores, and currency balances reside locally on your device storage. Uninstalling the app may delete local game data unless backed up via Google Play Games services.
